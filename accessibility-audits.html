A free accessibility audit for your Rails app.

We use your app the way a keyboard or screen reader user does, then tell you what's broken and where to fix it in your code: the view, partial, helper, or component. You don't get a URL and a CSS selector you have to trace back yourself.

Why it's free

A manual accessibility audit usually costs a few thousand dollars, and that's before anyone fixes anything. Plenty of teams never get one, so they never find out what's wrong.

We'd rather you find out. Auditing Rails apps also shows us which accessibility mistakes are common, and those become the posts on our blog. If you want help with the fixes afterward, we'd be glad to do it, but you don't have to hire us. The report is yours either way.

What we test

You pick up to three flows that matter most, like signing up, checking out, or whatever your users do every day. We test each one against WCAG 2.2 AA:

  • Keyboard only. Can you reach and use everything without a mouse? Can you see where focus is? Do modals and menus trap focus or lose it?
  • With VoiceOver. Do buttons, links, and form fields announce what they are? Are errors and Turbo updates announced? Do headings and landmarks make the page easy to navigate?
  • Visual checks. Color contrast, zooming to 200%, text spacing, and anything that relies on color alone.
  • Automated scans. To catch what a tool can find quickly, so we can spend our time on what it can't.

What you get

  • A prioritized list of issues. Most important first: anything that stops someone from finishing a flow, then anything that makes it harder.
  • The code to change. Each issue shows the fix in ERB or Phlex. If you share read access to your repo, we point at the exact file and line.
  • Fixes grouped by source. When one shared partial or helper causes the same problem on 40 pages, you see it as one fix, not 40 issues.
  • How to keep it fixed. Suggestions for system tests and lint checks, so the same issues don't come back.
  • A call to go through it. About 30 minutes with your team to walk through the findings and answer questions.

How it works

  1. Email us your app's URL and the flows you want tested. If they're behind a login, include a test account.
  2. If you can, give us read access to the repo. It's optional, but it makes the findings much more specific.
  3. We test and write up the findings, usually within two weeks.
  4. We go through the report together on a call.

What it isn't

  • It's not a compliance certificate. We don't produce a VPAT or an Accessibility Conformance Report, and we can't give legal advice. The audit covers the flows you choose, not your whole app.
  • It's not an overlay. We won't sell you a widget that promises to fix everything with one script tag. Accessibility gets fixed in your views.
  • It's only for Rails. We work only on Rails apps, which is why we can point at your code.
